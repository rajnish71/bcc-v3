# PURPOSE

This document defines the operational membership plans, eligibility, benefits, membership lifecycle notifications and configurable membership entitlements for the Bhopal Camera Club Unified Platform.

This document supplements MEM-006 by defining the practical implementation of operational memberships while preserving the constitutional separation between:

- Operational Memberships
- Constitutional Memberships
- Recognition Classes
- Status Overlays

MEM-006 remains the constitutional authority.

---

# DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Membership should encourage participation, recognize loyalty and provide value while remaining financially sustainable.

Benefits should primarily emphasize:

- Recognition
- Community Access
- Learning Opportunities
- Priority Access

rather than expensive physical benefits.

# STATUS OVERLAYS

Status Overlays supplement Membership Categories.

They never replace the underlying Membership.

Status Overlays are not Recognition Classes. A Status Overlay may coexist with an active Recognition Class and does not participate in the Single Active Recognition Rule (MEM-006 v1.1, Amendment 001).

Senior Member is the Status Overlay currently defined.

---

## Senior Member

Automatically awarded when ANY ONE of the following conditions is met:

- Age 60 years or above
- Age 50 years or above with five years of BCC Membership
- Ten years of BCC Membership irrespective of age

These eligibility conditions are constitutional (MEM-006 v1.1, SENIOR MEMBER STATUS). Years of BCC Membership means recognized actual BCC membership service (MEM-006 v1.1, BCC MEMBERSHIP TENURE).

Benefits

- Senior Member Badge
- Senior Member Recognition
- Unlimited Public Photographer Portfolio (recognition-based; Amendment 002 — RATIFIED)

# CONFIGURABLE BENEFITS

The platform shall not hard-code membership benefits.

The following shall be configurable through the Administrative Portal:

- Membership fees
- Membership validity
- Discount percentages
- Welcome Kit availability
- PVC Card availability
- Priority registration eligibility
- Public Photographer Portfolio entitlement
- Public Gallery entitlement
- Membership badges
- Recognition badges
- Membership email templates
- Membership notification triggers
- Activity eligibility
- Member-only resource access
- Any future configurable entitlement

Configuration changes shall apply prospectively and shall not require software deployment.

---

# CONSTITUTIONAL RULE

The BCC Unified Platform shall implement Membership Benefits through a configurable entitlement system rather than application code.

Membership categories shall define only the entitlement set assigned to a member.

No business logic, discount percentage, benefit, recognition, badge, notification template or configurable privilege shall be permanently hard-coded within the application.

All operational membership benefits shall be administered through the Administrative Portal.

This constitutional rule ensures that future amendments to membership plans, pricing, benefits or privileges may be implemented through administrative configuration without requiring changes to the platform source code.

The constitutional separation established by MEM-006 (for Recognition Classes and Status Overlays, formally through MEM-006 Amendment 001) between Operational Memberships, Constitutional Memberships, Recognition Classes and Status Overlays shall remain mandatory and shall not be violated by any future implementation.

# AMENDMENT 002

Recognition-Based Unlimited Public Photographer Portfolio

Amendment Number:
002

Authority:
Human Authority (Rajnish K. Khare) — policy decisions LOCKED

Recorded:
2026-10-09

Status:
APPROVED
FROZEN
AUTHORITATIVE

The policy decisions below were locked by Human Authority and the amendment text was ratified on 2026-10-09 (see Ratification Record below).

Purpose:
To make the Public Photographer Portfolio entitlement of the recognition-based statuses explicit, so that it does not depend on interpreting "Legacy Member Benefits" through Basic Membership inheritance, and to establish that it is unlimited.

Policy Changes:

1. Senior Member, Honorary Member, Honorary Mentor, Honorary Grandmaster and Honorary Senior Member each receive an Unlimited Public Photographer Portfolio entitlement.
2. This entitlement is recognition-based. It is an entitlement outcome of the applicable recognition status, assigned through the configurable entitlement system (CONFIGURABLE BENEFITS; CONSTITUTIONAL RULE), not through application code.
3. The entitlement is listed explicitly under each of the five statuses in the sections above. For Honorary Member, Honorary Mentor and Honorary Grandmaster, the "Legacy Member Benefits" reference is retained as a historical benefit reference; for these classes the Public Photographer Portfolio is Unlimited, and the Basic Membership portfolio maximum shall not be inherited through that reference.
4. Senior Member eligibility conditions are unchanged. No portfolio-related eligibility condition is added.

Affected Recognition Statuses:

- Senior Member (Status Overlay — classification unchanged)
- Honorary Member (Recognition Class — classification unchanged)
- Honorary Mentor (Recognition Class — classification unchanged)
- Honorary Grandmaster (Recognition Class — classification unchanged)
- Honorary Senior Member (Governance Recognition Class — classification unchanged)

Definition — Unlimited Public Photographer Portfolio:
No numeric maximum shall apply to the number of photographs that may be publicly visible in the member's Photographer Portfolio by virtue of this recognition-based entitlement.

It does not mean, and shall not be implemented as:

- automatic publication of the member's photo library
- automatic selection
- automatic promotion
- automatic ordering
- automatic trimming

The existing explicit portfolio-selection mechanism remains unchanged.

Active Membership Rule:
The recognition-based Unlimited Public Photographer Portfolio entitlement does not require an active underlying Operational or Constitutional Membership. Recognition status itself does not create Membership, and the constitutional distinction between Membership and Recognition is unchanged. Senior Member remains a Status Overlay; the Honorary Recognition Classes remain classified as stated in this document and MEM-006.

Individual Override Restriction:
An Individual Override or administrative restriction shall not reduce, cap or remove the recognition-based Unlimited Public Photographer Portfolio entitlement while the applicable recognition status remains valid. The existence and authority of Individual Overrides generally are unchanged; this amendment establishes only that they cannot be used to reduce this recognition-based entitlement.

Public Gallery Separation:
This amendment does not grant Public Gallery entitlement to any status, including Senior Member and Honorary Senior Member. The Public Photographer Portfolio and the Public Gallery remain separate configurable entitlements.

Photographer Directory Separation:
This amendment does not modify Photographer Directory eligibility or any provision of PROFILE-ARCH-001.

Portfolio-Selection Preservation:
Photo selection (including photos.portfolio_selected) and its behaviour are unchanged. Photo ownership and copyright rules are unchanged.

Unchanged Provisions:

- Basic Member: Public Photographer Portfolio maximum 5 photos visible to public.
- Student Member: Public Photographer Portfolio maximum 10 photos visible to public.
- Public Gallery rules, Showcase rules and Photographer Directory eligibility.
- All membership plans, fees, validity, numbering, RBAC, activity eligibility, notifications and every other benefit.
- The ADOPTION RECORD and CONFORMING AMENDMENT 001, preserved as historical records.
- MEM-006 and MEM-007, which are not modified.
