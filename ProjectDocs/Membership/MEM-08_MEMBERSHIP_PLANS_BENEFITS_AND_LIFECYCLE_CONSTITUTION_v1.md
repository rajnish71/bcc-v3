# PURPOSE

This document defines the operational membership plans, eligibility, benefits, membership lifecycle notifications and configurable membership entitlements for the Bhopal Camera Club Unified Platform.

This document supplements MEM-006 by defining the practical implementation of operational memberships while preserving the constitutional separation between:

- Operational Memberships
- Constitutional Memberships
- Recognition Classes
- Status Overlays

MEM-006 remains the constitutional authority.

---

# DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Membership should encourage participation, recognize loyalty and provide value while remaining financially sustainable.

Benefits should primarily emphasize:

- Recognition
- Community Access
- Learning Opportunities
- Priority Access

rather than expensive physical benefits.

Membership notifications shall use a common branded email template.

Template placeholders include:

- Member Name
- Membership Category
- Previous Membership
- Membership Number

- Membership Valid Until
- Benefits Summary
- Primary Call-to-Action

# CONFIGURABLE BENEFITS

The platform shall not hard-code membership benefits.

The following shall be configurable through the Administrative Portal:

- Membership fees
- Membership validity
- Discount percentages
- Welcome Kit availability
- PVC Card availability
- Priority registration eligibility
- Public Photographer Portfolio entitlement
- Public Gallery entitlement
- Membership badges
- Recognition badges
- Membership email templates
- Membership notification triggers
- Activity eligibility
- Member-only resource access
- Any future configurable entitlement

Configuration changes shall apply prospectively and shall not require software deployment.

---

# CONSTITUTIONAL RULE

The BCC Unified Platform shall implement Membership Benefits through a configurable entitlement system rather than application code.

Membership categories shall define only the entitlement set assigned to a member.

No business logic, discount percentage, benefit, recognition, badge, notification template or configurable privilege shall be permanently hard-coded within the application.

All operational membership benefits shall be administered through the Administrative Portal.

This constitutional rule ensures that future amendments to membership plans, pricing, benefits or privileges may be implemented through administrative configuration without requiring changes to the platform source code.

The constitutional separation established by MEM-006 between Operational Memberships, Constitutional Memberships, Recognition Classes and Status Overlays shall remain mandatory and shall not be violated by any future implementation.
