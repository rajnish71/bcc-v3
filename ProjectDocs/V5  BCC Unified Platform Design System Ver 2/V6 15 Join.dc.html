Architectural Decision Record

Core decisions that govern this page. Every subsequent design choice derives from these mandates. Where a section design appears to violate any of these, the ADR takes precedence.

The Most Important Design Principle for This Page

Design this page as the first impression of the Bhopal Camera Club community.
Not as a membership sales page.

Visitors should leave thinking: "I want to be part of this community." — not — "I should buy this membership."

Page Scope

Responsibility Ends at Registration

This page's responsibility ends when the visitor clicks "Become a Member" and begins the registration journey. Membership management, renewal, and tier upgrades belong to the Member Hub. This page does not own those flows.

Boundary
Guest state → /join → "Become a Member" → /auth/register

Design Decision Rationale

Every major decision justified against the design authority documents and the page philosophy. Implementation must not override these decisions without an explicit design authority amendment.

DEC-01

"Belong Here." — not "Join BCC"

"Join BCC" is a command. "Belong Here." is an identity statement. The hero headline describes what membership feels like, not what it requires. This is the single highest-leverage copywriting decision on the page — it sets the emotional register for everything that follows. The period after "Here." is deliberate: it is an assertion, not an invitation.

DEC-02

Dark Hero with Community Photography

The dark (#141210) hero follows the canonical "dark hero overlay" pattern. It creates visual weight and emotional seriousness appropriate for a membership invitation — not a product page. The community photograph (right panel) shows people, not photographs. This is the clearest possible visual statement of the page's philosophy: you are joining a community of photographers, not a platform of photographs.

DEC-03

Gold CTA Placement — Hero vs. Final Section

The constitution mandates one gold CTA per view. "Explore Memberships" in the hero is a navigational action — it anchors to §04. "Become a Member" at the end is the conversion action. Only the final CTA triggers the registration journey. The hero CTA uses the gold gradient to signal primacy within the hero's view — but since these are in different viewport positions, they are never simultaneously gold in the same screen. The final "Become a Member" CTA in the dark section is the authoritative primary action of this page.

DEC-04

Identity Cards Before Comparison Table

Showing the comparison table first (the default in most membership pages) puts price and features at the centre of the decision. Showing identity cards first puts the visitor at the centre. The visitor self-identifies, receives a recommendation, and then confirms the details in the comparison. This sequence transforms a purchase decision into a recognition moment.

DEC-05

No Feature Matrix in Comparison

A checkbox feature matrix (Common in SaaS pricing pages) creates a shopping mindset — it implies the visitor is choosing between competing products. BCC memberships are not products. The row-per-tier editorial format — leading with "Who is this for?" — frames the decision as finding the right home, not finding the best deal. Benefits are shown on expand, not as a comparison axis.

DEC-06

Recognition Classes Excluded

Full, Life, Patron, and Founding memberships are not open to new applicants. Showing them alongside operational tiers creates confusion and sets false expectations. Their absence is intentional, not accidental. A new applicant cannot become a Life member by applying — including these tiers would be actively misleading. This is a MEM-006 mandate, not a design preference.
